## English

“Implemented” describes software, not an accepted physical device. IDs labelled observed come from the cited dated record; owner configuration, expected IDs, acquisition plans and unverified paths are separate. No model is identified from a generic PCI ID alone. The [scope](release-scope-0.4.0.md) determines reference devices; [historical status](hardware-verification-status.md) retains original results.

The Ryzen video IMG_7653 (9.0s) visibly records10EC:8168 rev26, BDF0700 (07:00.0), but not subsystem/BAR/command/TxConfig. Its deployed EFI59a701b1… is associated by deployment provenance, not a hash read from video. This observation does not identify RTL8111H or prove traffic. NUC5 IMG_7654 records8086:15A3 rev03,00:19.0 and a software-NVM refusal. The original captured evidence and its interpretation remain bounded by those exact builds.
